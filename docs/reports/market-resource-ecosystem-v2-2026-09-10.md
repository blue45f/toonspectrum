# Market Resource Ecosystem V2

## Goal
The market is a scene-production workspace, not a file-format catalog.

## Primary journey
1. Start scene structure with a template.
2. Fill the scene with 2D assets.
3. Add perspective or drawing references with 3D when needed.
4. Refine line, paint and texture with brushes.
5. Finish mood and consistency with palette/filter resources.

## Progressive disclosure
- Templates and 2D assets are visually promoted as the primary starting points.
- Each family shows three common subcategories first.
- Remaining specialist categories are available through an explicit disclosure control.
- Advanced compatibility and license details remain available but do not block initial discovery.

## Cross-resource continuity
Every resource detail page exposes the next three useful resource families while preserving the current genre/theme tag. A template therefore leads to assets, brushes and finishing resources; an asset leads back to a suitable scene template and forward to finishing tools.

## UX contract
- Never require users to know internal engine names before browsing.
- Preserve exact URL filters so back/forward and sharing retain discovery state.
- Keep Studio as the execution destination, not a separate dead end.
- Keep licensing and compatibility visible before installation or use.
- Maintain keyboard focus, 44px touch targets and reduced-motion behavior.
